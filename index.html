? How to Play

🎹 Three Play Modes

Switch between modes using the buttons in the top bar.

💻 Keyboard
Use your computer keyboard — A B C D E F G play notes. Best for laptops & desktops
⌨️ Touch
On-screen QWERTY pad for touchscreens — best for phones & tablets
🎹 Piano
Tap or click the piano keys directly — no keyboard needed. Best for tablets used as a piano
⌨️ Keyboard Mode

The keys A B C D E F G play the notes of the same name in the current octave.

A→ A
B→ B
C→ C
D→ D
E→ E
F→ F
G→ G
Shift + key
Play the sharp (♯) of that note — e.g. Shift+C = C♯
Alt + key
Play the flat (♭) of that note — e.g. Alt+D = D♭

Hold Shift for sharp (♯), Alt for flat (♭).

📋 Song Book

Enable Song Book in Settings to see a scrolling strip of notes to play along with. The highlighted note is the one to play next. Tap any note in the strip to jump to it.

Use the tempo controls to speed up or slow down the guide. Each song remembers its last tempo for next time. 23 songs included — from Twinkle Twinkle to Chopsticks.

Some songs have chords — two notes played at the same time, shown as F+G. Press both together to advance.

📱 Mobile Tips

On iPhone, rotate to landscape for a wider keyboard and more comfortable playing. Portrait mode works well for quick practice. Make sure your ringer switch is not muted — it silences Web Audio on iOS.
